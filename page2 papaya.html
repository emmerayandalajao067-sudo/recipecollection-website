NUTRI PAPAYA CHIPS

Masarap, Mura, Masustansyang Pagkain

⏱ Prep: 1 Hour 🍳 Cook: 30 Mins 📦 Yield: 20 ₱35.00

What You'll Need:

  • 1pc Unripe, Nearly Ripe, and Ripe Papaya
  • 1 Cup Flour & 1 tbsp + 1/2 tsp Cornstarch
  • 2 Tbsp Crispy Fry Mix
  • 400ml Oil (for frying)
  • 1/4 tsp Pepper
  • 1 tbsp Sour Cream Flavoring

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Prep: Wash, clean, and slice papayas into thin pieces.
  2. Dry: Sun-dry the slices to reduce moisture.
  3. Batter: Mix flour, crispy fry mix, cornstarch, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  4. Fry: Coat slices in mixture and fry in hot oil until golden brown.
  5. Flavor: Let chips rest, then shake with sour cream powder in a container.
  6. Finish: Package and serve!
