Cucumber Milk Tea

A Refreshing Twist on a Classic Favorite

Ingredients

  • 1 black tea bag
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 cup all-purpose cream
  • 1 cup fresh milk
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp sugar

Preparation Guide

01. Peel, slice, and blend the cucumber.
02. Mix with water, sugar, and lemon juice.
03. Strain the cucumber mixture.
04. Steep black tea and mix with juice.
05. Add milk and cream, then serve!
