Jackfruit Seed Filled Hopia

A sustainable and healthy alternative to traditional fillings

Prep Time 1.5 Hours
Cook Time 30 Mins
Yield 30 Pcs
Price ₱10.00

Ingredients

Filling

  • Jackfruit Seeds 250g
  • Vegetable Oil 113g
  • Brown Sugar 110g
  • Vanilla Extract 30g

Dough (1st & 2nd)

  • Flour (Total) 188g
  • Butter/Shortening 114g
  • Sugar/Salt As specified

Product Description

Hopia is a beloved Filipino snack. As researchers, we were inspired to innovate using jackfruit seeds as a sustainable, nutritious alternative to traditional fillings.

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash jackfruit seeds and boil until tender.
  2. Peel off the thick layer of skin.
  3. Sauté seeds until golden brown, then blend until powdery.
  4. Mix with sugar, vanilla, and oil until desired texture is achieved.
  1. 1st Dough: Combine ingredients until a ball forms; rest under a wet towel.
  2. 2nd Dough: Combine in a separate bowl until smooth.
  3. Lamination: Roll 1st dough flat, spread 2nd dough on top, fold 3 times to create layers.
  1. Roll dough into a cylinder and cut into 12 equal parts (or as needed for 30 pcs).
  2. Flatten dough, add a scoop of jackfruit filling, and pinch to close.
  3. Apply egg wash and bake for 15-20 minutes until golden brown.
